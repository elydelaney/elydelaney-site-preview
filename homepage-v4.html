The real problem

Most founders think this is a marketing problem.
Better ads. A rebrand. New brand colors. It is not.

The real story, the real mission, the real personality of the company usually lives in one person’s head. The founder’s. It never makes it into what sales says on a call, what marketing puts in an ad, or what HR tells a new hire on day one.

Every department fills that gap on its own, guessing at what sounds right. Marketing feels like it is not working. Sales is not converting. HR cannot keep good people. Four departments, four problems, four plans to fix them. Not one voice underneath.

Common questions

What do you actually do?

I’m a communications strategist. I find the voice that’s already sitting inside a company, usually in the founder, and get sales, marketing, PR, social, and HR all saying it. For coaches and consultants, I run a lighter version of the same process through the Follow Up Rockstar program, matched to individual follow up.

What’s the A3 Framework?

Three steps. Audit: find where the story splits, department by department. Align: sit down with the founder, pull the real voice, build one Brand Voice Profile. Amplify: roll it out, sales and social first, then inward to HR.

Do you work with people just starting out?

Yes. The free Follow Up Fix email course is the best first step. If you want something done for you, the "Follow Up Jumpstart" is the right entry point.

Do you do speaking engagements?

Absolutely. I speak to business associations, mastermind groups, chambers of commerce, and corporate teams on the voice problem behind most marketing and sales breakdowns. Head to the Speaking page for topics and availability.
