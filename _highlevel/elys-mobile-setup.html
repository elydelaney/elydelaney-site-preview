My Mobile Tech Stack For Speakers

As a speaker, we’re often doing virtual presentations from hotel rooms and trying to get work done at coffee shops. If you’re like me, I have an amazing command center in my office, and it’s just not the same when I’m out and about traveling. In order to create the best environment and be as productive as I can while traveling, I’ve put together my own Mobile Tech Stack For Speakers that’s worked pretty well for me. I don’t go as far as bringing a green screen with me everywhere I go, but the rest of the tools keep me looking as professional as possible on the go.
