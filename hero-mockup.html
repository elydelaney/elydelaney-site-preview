Common Questions

You probably have a few questions.

What do you actually do?

I build automated email follow up systems for professional service providers. Think consultants, coaches, CPAs, attorneys, financial advisors. People who have great conversations but lose the follow through. I fix that — with a system that runs on its own.

How is this different from just sending a newsletter?

A newsletter goes to everyone at the same time. An automated follow up system meets people where they are in the relationship — and follows up based on that. It feels personal because it is designed to be personal.

Do you work with people who are just starting out?

Yes. The free Follow-Up Fix email series is a good first step. If you want something done for you, the "Follow Up Jumpstart" is the right entry point.
